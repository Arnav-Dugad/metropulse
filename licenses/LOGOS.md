# Operator logos and line symbols

MetroPulse displays the logos and line symbols of the transit operators listed below so riders can recognise their system.
All marks are the property of their respective owners and may be trademarks or registered trademarks. **MetroPulse is an independent app and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any of these operators.**

Every file comes from Wikimedia Commons under the licence shown and is unmodified apart from removing editor metadata. Public-domain status on Commons (`PD-textlogo`, `PD-shape`: below the threshold of originality) covers copyright only; trademark rights still apply.
